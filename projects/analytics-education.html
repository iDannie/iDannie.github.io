AI MOVEMENT / SEPTEMBER 2022 — AUGUST 2024

Making analytics approachable.

As a Data Analyst and Instructor at AI Movement, I developed and delivered practical training that helped learners build confidence with analytical tools.

Topics and delivery

  • Excel for data analysis, Python, and SQL.
  • Hands-on exercises, projects, and real-world examples.
  • Training materials tailored to different experience levels.
  • Learner assessments to identify gaps and improve the training experience.
  • Collaboration with stakeholders to understand training needs.

Teaching philosophy

Connect each technique to a question. Give learners space to practice, then help them explain the insight in their own words.

The code shown on the portfolio card is an illustrative teaching motif.
