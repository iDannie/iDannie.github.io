Product Order Fulfilment

Delivered, returned, lost and back-ordered product analysis.

Analysis focus

Compare fulfilment outcomes by product and month while keeping monetary values and quantities separate.

Report features

  • Status cards use summed LineTotal monetary values
  • The product-by-status percentage matrix uses OrderQty within each product row
  • Separate charts compare LineTotal and OrderQty by order status

Interpreting the report

The report-definition comparison confirms that quantity shares and monetary shares use different fields. Neither should be described as the percentage of distinct orders. Compare both to identify products needing closer operational review.
