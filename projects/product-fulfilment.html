Product Order Fulfilment

Explore delivered, returned, lost and back-ordered products.

The question

How do order outcomes vary across products and product lines?

The approach

Start with the overall picture, select a period or product, and compare the filtered view. Check the active filters and metric definitions before interpreting a result.

Inside the report

  • Home-page navigation to the analysis page
  • Delivered, returned, lost and back-order views
  • Order-date and product filters
  • Status trends and product-line breakdowns

Quantities, order counts and line totals are distinct measures. Status percentages need a consistent denominator; distinct-order calculations require a Desktop check.
