Spotify Track Explorer

Track popularity, release patterns and audio characteristics.

Analysis focus

Explore which tracks lead on recorded streams and compare their release dates and audio features. Artist, track and date selections define the comparison scope.

Report features

  • Artist, track and date filters
  • Stream rankings and release-date distributions
  • Audio characteristics for the top-stream selection

Interpreting the report

The date visuals describe track releases, not daily listening. Stream totals represent the stored dataset snapshot; they do not demonstrate that an audio characteristic causes greater popularity.
