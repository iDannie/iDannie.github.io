MONIEPOINT INC. / SEPTEMBER 2024 — SEPTEMBER 2025

Business performance, made visible.

As a Performance Analyst at Moniepoint, I worked with regional management and stakeholders to define business performance metrics and make operational data easier to act on.

The work

  • Defined and monitored KPIs with business stakeholders.
  • Developed performance measurement frameworks using quantitative and qualitative data.
  • Designed interactive dashboards for daily performance tracking.
  • Analyzed trends and tested hypotheses about services and products.
  • Produced recurring reports with recommendations for management.

The approach

Start with the business question, agree on the metric, examine the evidence, and communicate findings in a form decision-makers can use.
