Project walkthrough

Business Insight 360

Explore finance, sales, marketing and supply-chain performance.

The question

Where do sales, margins and forecast performance differ across products, customers and markets?

The approach

Start with the overall picture, select a period or product, and compare the filtered view. Check the active filters and metric definitions before interpreting a result.

Inside the report

  • Finance: profit-and-loss reporting and benchmarks
  • Sales: customer and product comparisons
  • Marketing: regional and product performance
  • Supply chain: forecast accuracy, net error and risk

The file contains four analytical pages plus a home page. An Executive View is not included. The report is a portfolio exercise; company branding does not establish a client relationship.
