Apple Product Performance

Product sales, quantities and year-over-year comparisons.

Analysis focus

Compare sales value and units across products, months and countries, with previous-year measures to support performance review.

Report features

  • Product, country, order and date selections
  • Sales value and quantity cards
  • Previous-year sales and year-over-year indicators

Interpreting the report

Quantity and sales value answer different questions. A year-over-year comparison requires aligned periods and a valid date relationship; rounded card totals alone cannot verify the percentage.
